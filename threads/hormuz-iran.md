*Current as of 17 September 2026 · wire-sourced*

#### State of play

War began 28 February 2026 with US and Israeli strikes on Iranian military-industrial targets. A ceasefire held from June; Trump declared it over in July and fighting resumed. Iran operates a restricted shipping zone in the strait and attacks vessels transiting outside its terms. Gulf–Iran talks were postponed on 14 September. China's foreign minister publicly urged Iran and the US to show restraint and revive talks (Reuters, 16 Sept) — Beijing's most forward public positioning on the war so far.

#### The disputed fact, now resolved

Reuters vessel-tracking data, 16 September: **four transits** , two in and two out, down from seven the previous day, against a ten-day average of eighteen. No VLCCs and no LNG carriers. Some vessels run with transponders off, so the figure understates. Bab al-Mandeb ran 22, down from 24.

This supersedes the position held in the 15 September brief, which treated single-digit transits as a thinly-sourced claim and gave Washington's two-thirds-of-pre-war-flows figure a straighter reading. The tracking data is the better evidence. **War-risk insurance remains the tiebreaker** : premiums went from 0.25% to 3–10% of hull value.

#### The re-plumbing

Saudi Aramco is offering Asian refiners crude by ship-to-ship transfer off **Sohar, Oman** , outside the strait, and has roughly doubled Gulf loadings at Ras Tanura and Juaymah to about 4 million barrels a day; four VLCCs carrying 8 million barrels loaded at Ras Tanura on Wednesday. Yanbu is suspended and some September European cargoes face cancellation. The East–West pipeline was shut on Friday after drone damage.

**Wire conflict, unresolved:** Reuters reports the US energy secretary saying the pipeline should be back within days; AP reports weeks. Both readings stand. Riyadh's re-routing behaviour suggests it is not planning around days.

#### The cost

CBO, 15 September: **$38 billion through 1 August** , rising roughly $3 billion a month; 18 US service members killed; munitions the dominant expense; up to **five years to replenish US stockpiles** ; Hegseth has requested about $90 billion in emergency restocking. CBO expects roughly 0.5% added inflation in the first quarter of 2027. Iraq, for scale, ran about $2 trillion over eight years.

#### What would count as real change

  * Transits recovering into double digits for a sustained week, or premiums falling below roughly 1%.
  * The East–West pipeline returning — and which wire turns out to have been right.
  * Gulf–Iran talks actually convening after the postponement.
  * A US decision to fund the restock, which would be the signal that Washington is planning for the Pacific rather than the Gulf.
