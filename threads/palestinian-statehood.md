# Thread: Palestinian statehood and the E1 corridor
*Opened 17 September 2026 · wire-sourced*

Cool

#### State of play

Rights groups report a new tender adding **2,167 housing units** to the E1 settlement plan east of Jerusalem. Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich has said the project would "bury" a Palestinian state; Mahmoud Abbas condemned it as a violation of international law. Separately, the State Department denied visas to Palestinian officials for a second consecutive year, keeping Abbas from the UN General Assembly.

#### Why it is structural

E1 is the single build in this conflict that is decisive **geographically** rather than politically. It severs the northern West Bank from the southern and cuts both from East Jerusalem, removing territorial contiguity from any future settlement. Nearly everything else about the two-state question is reversible by a signature. This part is reversible only by demolition.

Note the sourcing convention, which is typical of the beat: settlement counts reach the wires through rights groups rather than government announcement, which is why the numbers are always attributed and occasionally disputed.

#### What would count as real change

  * Ground-breaking rather than tendering — the gap between the two is where reversal lives.
  * Any US response beyond a statement.
  * European recognition moves timed against the construction.
  * Palestinian Authority collapse or dissolution, which would remove the counterparty entirely.
