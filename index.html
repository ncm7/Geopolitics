What this is. One brief each morning: what moved, why it matters structurally, and the concept each event teaches. Concepts are filed the day an event makes them necessary, so the ledger is a curriculum written by what the world actually did rather than by a syllabus decided in advance.

How it is sourced. Reuters and the Associated Press are the default and the tiebreaker. Center-rated outlets come second, checked against Ground News at the time of use. Anything else is used only when it is the only source carrying a story, and is labeled inline with its lean. Where a wire and a non-wire source disagree, the wire wins and the disagreement is stated.

Corrections are marked inside the day's brief, never by rewriting history.
